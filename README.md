EMBERLINE — a round-based tactical first-person shooter in the style Counter-Strike made famous: two teams, short rounds won by eliminating the other team or by planting and defusing a charge, money between rounds to buy weapons, and gunfire where accuracy counts more than speed. Single-player against bots on an original dusk container-port map, built on three.js with no build step and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, Ctrl to crouch, Shift to walk, Space to jump, B for the buy menu, E to plant or defuse, G to throw a grenade, 1-5 to switch weapons.
